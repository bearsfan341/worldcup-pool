# Wolves of Fantasy — League History (2015–2025)

League ID 684189, ESPN Fantasy Football. Eleven complete seasons of data pulled directly from ESPN (see `/fantasy/data` for the raw CSVs and `/fantasy/data/DATA_GAPS.txt` for known limitations). Team names churn every year — the league treats a fresh, filthier team name as a birthright — so every number below is tied to the **manager**, not the team name, using ESPN's stable per-manager team slot across seasons.

Two people have cycled through the league: **Phil Lannon** played 2015–2016 and then left; **Andrew Campbell** joined in 2018 to fill the vacancy, and the league has run as a steady 12-team league in every season except 2017 (11 teams, the one year the league ran a man down after Phil's departure).

## League Records

Single-game and streak records pulled from all 1,026 matchups on file, 2015–2025.

**Highest score ever:** Derek Booker, 211.8, week 15 of 2025 — a playoff game against Johnny Marcks, the round before he won the title.

**Lowest score ever:** Johnny Marcks, 28.0, week 2 of 2015 — in a loss to Derek Booker (151.0).

**Biggest blowout:** Derek Booker 211.8, Johnny Marcks 66.88 — week 15 of 2025, a 144.92-point margin. The same game that set the all-time high score also set the all-time blowout record.

**Closest game(s):** Five exact ties are on record, all from 2015–2017. The standout: Angelo Hernandez 66, Johnny Marcks 66 — week 11 of 2015.

**Most points scored in a loss:** Corey Ploss, 164.48, week 5 of 2021 — and still lost, to Johnny Marcks' 186.52.

**Fewest points scored in a win:** Jarett Coy, 54.0, week 14 of 2015 — edged Pj Lannon's 52.0.

**Longest win streak:** Johnny Marcks, 11 straight regular-season games, week 5 of 2023 through week 1 of 2024.

**Longest losing streak:** Alex Topham, 9 straight regular-season games, week 3 through week 11 of 2022.

Johnny Marcks shows up in six of these eight records — the lowest score ever, both ends of the highest-score/biggest-blowout game, the closest game, the win in the highest-scoring heartbreaker anyone's ever lost, and the longest win streak in league history. Derek Booker is next with three appearances (he's on the other side of two of Marcks' entries). Nobody else shows up more than once.

## Season-by-Season Recaps

**2015** (12 teams) — Champion: **Jarett Coy** (Team Coy) def. Tyler Scarlett 72–47 in the final. Derek Booker led the league in regular-season points (1,387.0) but finished 6th. Dakota Bender finished last.

**2016** (12 teams) — Champion: **Jarett Coy** (BACK 2 BACK FFL WORLD CHAMP — the team name was not modest) def. Johnny Marcks 93–72, back-to-back titles. Pj Lannon led in points (1,300.0). Phil Lannon finished last in his final season before leaving the league.

**2017** (11 teams — Phil Lannon departed, league ran a man down) — Champion: **Tyler Scarlett** (Saving Ryan's Privates) edged Derek Booker 119–117. Alex Topham led in points (1,079.0) but finished 6th. Jarett Coy — the reigning "back to back" champion — finished dead last the very next year.

**2018** (12 teams — Andrew Campbell joins, back to a full 12) — Champion: **Alex Topham** (Pjs Japanese PeePee Pats) topped Jarett Coy 150.38–133.1. Andrew Campbell finished last in his rookie season. First season with transaction-log data available.

**2019** (12 teams) — Champion: **Andrew Campbell** (Naked Keg Stand) def. Pj Lannon 128.26–114.38 — worst-to-first from his 2018 debut. Angelo Hernandez led in points (1,266.7). Dakota Bender finished last.

**2020** (12 teams, COVID season) — Champion: **Andrew Campbell** (Naked Keg Stand) repeats, routing Dustin Gamble 165.36–102.14 in the final. Dustin Gamble led in regular-season points (1,319.42) but lost the title game. Jarett Coy finished last.

**2021** (12 teams) — Champion: **Jarett Coy** (Ships' Out For The Coys) outlasted Derek Booker in the closest final in league history, 116.52–116.06 (0.46 points). Corey Ploss led the league in points (1,838.26). Andrew Campbell finished last.

**2022** (12 teams, playoff field trimmed to 6) — Champion: **Pj Lannon** (WADDDLE ON MY W OOD) def. Johnny Marcks 128.62–122.46. Pj Lannon also led the league in points (1,817.18) — a rare wire-to-wire year. Alex Topham finished last.

**2023** (12 teams) — Champion: **Angelo Hernandez** (Sun God Squad) survived a 150.04–149.58 nail-biter over Derek Booker — the second title in three years decided by under half a point. Johnny Marcks led in points (1,828.74) but missed the final. Corey Ploss finished last.

**2024** (12 teams) — Champion: **Dustin Gamble** (Slim Nickens) blew out Tyler Scarlett 136.06–91.12, also leading the league in points (1,824.36) — a genuine wire-to-wire title. Pj Lannon finished last, one year after playing in the 2022 final.

**2025** (12 teams) — Champion: **Derek Booker** (Wheaton Warbirds) beat Angelo Hernandez 146.02–106.18, also leading the league in points (1,896.22, an all-time league high). Dustin Gamble — the defending champion — finished last, completing a title-to-cellar collapse. See `SEASON_PREVIEW.md` for the full account of how unlucky that collapse actually was.

## Data Notes & Gaps

- Draft results, standings, and full weekly matchup/playoff data are complete for all 11 seasons (2015–2025).
- **ESPN's transaction log (trades, waiver moves, free-agent adds) is only available from 2018 onward.** 2015, 2016, and 2017 have no trade or waiver data — ESPN does not retain or expose it for those seasons through the API, public or authenticated. This is a hard platform limitation, not a gap in the pull.
- **Even within 2018+, ESPN has pruned the full contents of most trades from 2022 onward.** We have complete details (players exchanged) for 27 trades. We also found hard evidence — via orphaned roster-cleanup records that reference a trade ESPN no longer serves the details for — that at least 48 additional trades happened: 3 in 2022, 9 in 2023, 20 in 2024, and 16 in 2025. For those, we can usually identify one manager involved but not the counterparty or players. See `SEASON_PREVIEW.md`'s Trade Rankings section for the full breakdown — every trade-related ranking and superlative there is a floor, not a final count.
- 2017 ran with 11 teams (Phil Lannon left after 2016; Andrew Campbell didn't join until 2018), so all 2017 records/points are on an 11-team basis.
- The 2026 season exists on ESPN but has **no draft and empty rosters as of this pull (2026-08-12)** — the draft hasn't happened yet. See `SEASON_PREVIEW.md` for what that means for the 2026 outlook.
